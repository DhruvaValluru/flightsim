What every run gives you

The rendered clip with its honesty panel • a live aero side panel (angle of attack, lift/drag, stall margin, wind in the FDM) • the “what the conditions did” report — your flight vs the same flight in still air • full telemetry + provenance downloads — every number traceable to what you asked for.

What it refuses (on purpose)

Speeds below stall or beyond the tables • altitudes that hit terrain (checked under the wingtips, not just the fuselage) • unknown surfaces/aircraft/places (it asks for coordinates rather than guessing) • unbaked terrain (it offers to bake) • weather data it cannot fetch • renders where frames show nothing. Every refusal is named. Not modelled and stated: airframe damage, icing, rain physics, debris, wet runways.
