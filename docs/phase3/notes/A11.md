- A. Raise `PassError("labels.no_bundle")` from a helper called by both callers (chosen): reuses the handlers both callers already have, one place for the rule, headless untouched because the helper is only reached after a render.
- B. Make `attach_engine_labels` itself raise. Simpler call sites, but it is also called on re-runs and by tests/test_annotation_passes.py:492 with hand-built bundles, and it would make "no render requested" ambiguous; rejected.
- C. A new `LabelBundleError` class. Needs new handlers in two callers for no gain; rejected.
- Engine: fix the specific failure found (chosen) vs. a rewrite of the writer; the writer exists, so rewrite is unjustified until step 1 says otherwise.
