On the A-4 at 1280 px: wingtips projected at 521.5 and 758.5, mask 499..753
(22 px further left, 5 px short on the right). Every number-against-number
check passes on the same frames (`label_geometry` re-projects the keypoints
to 0.0000 px, `landmark_reprojection` matches the engine's own projections
to 0.00 px, `pose_matches_spec`, `intrinsics_match_spec`, `projection_matrix`
all PASS); only the one check that compares numbers with PIXELS fails. The
engine's reported projection and the engine's rendered image therefore
disagree by a horizontal offset of about 0.6 deg of view (1.1 % of a
54.4 deg horizontal field): the rendering camera is yawed, or the rendered
image's principal point is off-centre, relative to the projection the engine
reports and the verifier reproduces. Which of the two is the question the
fix has to answer, by rendering a frame with a scene point of known
position on the image centre and reading where its pixels land. The
candidates are the render commandlet's camera setup against the projection
it writes (`FlightSimRenderCommandlet.cpp`, ue/, Windows scope) and, less
likely since three independent projections agree, the verifier's intrinsics
(`core/capture/verify.py`, Mac scope). Decision: the Windows builder takes
the diagnosis first; if the engine's render matches its own projection and
the verifier is the odd one out, the packet goes to the Mac.
