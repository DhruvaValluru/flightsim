## Clauses redefined, for review

- **Range extinction.** Was colour distance from the sky above each peak, both
  peaks in one frame. Now the p10-p90 luminance spread inside the rock, the far
  peak from a `-HideNearTerrain` render, at the scene's default fog. Why: the far
  peak's pixel lay on the near copy, and sky distance was not monotonic in fog.
  New control: `-NoAerialPerspective` must miss the threshold.
- **Extinction follows visibility.** Was a far/near ratio drop of 0.10. Now the
  near peak alone across the 50 km and 10 km days, kept at most 0.75. Why: the
  far peak sits on the fog's opacity cap on both days. The 0.10 is retired, 0.75
  is reused from the range clause. Its bad value, 0.93 kept at thin fog, is from
  a builder script on renders since overwritten, so it is weaker evidence.
- **Exposure metric discriminates.** Control unchanged in kind: auto-exposure
  past 16. New is the rig: `-GroundDarkAt=10` darkens the plain mid-shot, and the
  same step under manual must hold under 8.
- **Cloud base bracket.** Shadows on, `-NoCloudShadows` removed. The ground is
  compared after its mean shade is divided out, so the shadow is not counted as
  cloud. New `cloud_min_ground_kept` 0.25: a stated floor, between 0.86 good and
  0.01 for the defect, not a measured line.
- **Verdict.** New state INCOMPLETE, exit 3: a missing side-by-side is not a pass.

## How the clauses came to pass

- **Aircraft ground shadow:** engine fixes, threshold untouched. The flat ground
  and raster were wound face-down on 5.7 and culled, so no ground existed to take
  a shadow (863 px once rewound). With the sky light captured it reads 1243, near
  the 5.5 record of 1181. The "VSM softness" explanation in packet B4-3 was wrong.
- **Exposure metric:** auto-exposure on 5.7 works. The old flight's frame barely
  changed, so the meter had nothing to chase; a real step gives 30.22.
- **Cloud shadows:** the sky light never ran (real-time capture returns at once
  for a scene capture). A one-time capture before the first frame fixes it.
