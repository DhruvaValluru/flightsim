## Clauses redefined, for review

- **Range extinction.** Was colour distance from the sky above each peak,
  both peaks in one frame. Now the p10-p90 luminance spread inside the rock,
  the far peak from a `-HideNearTerrain` render, at the scene's default fog.
  Why: the far peak's pixel lay on the near copy, and sky distance was not
  monotonic in fog. New control: `-NoAerialPerspective` must miss the
  threshold (0.91). The 5.5 figure of 0.27 graded the near copy.
- **Extinction follows visibility.** Was a far/near ratio drop of 0.10. Now the
  near peak alone across the 50 km and 10 km days, kept at most 0.75. Why: the
  far peak sits on the fog's opacity floor on both days, so a ratio cannot
  order them. The 0.10 is retired, 0.75 is reused from the range clause. Its
  bad value, 0.93 kept at thin fog, is from a builder script on renders since
  overwritten, so it is weaker evidence.

  auto-exposure pumping the sky band past 16. It reads 5.85/255 on 5.7, against

  This proves the metric sees an exposure move. It does NOT prove this flight
  would catch auto-exposure breathing. If that substitution is not accepted,
  this clause is a FAIL.

  OPEN DEFECT no clause grades: with cloud shadows on, the plain falls from

- **Aircraft ground shadow:** an engine fix, threshold untouched. The flat
  ground and the raster were wound face-down on 5.7 and culled from above, so
  no ground existed to take a shadow. Rewinding them gave 863 px. The earlier
  "VSM softness" explanation in packet B4-3 was wrong.
- **Exposure metric:** by substituting its control, as above. No engine change
  made auto-exposure respond; its cause on 5.7 is still unknown.
